# AI Tutoring Agency

An ethical AI-powered tutoring and study assistant designed to help students **learn**, not just get answers.

This project focuses on building a sustainable AI tutoring service that uses Socratic questioning, guided hints, and personalized support — while strictly avoiding contract cheating and academic dishonesty.

---

## ⚠️ Why This Approach?

Services that simply complete homework for students face serious legal and ethical risks (contract cheating laws, platform bans, etc.).

This project takes the safer and more valuable path:

- Never gives the final answer directly
- Uses Socratic questioning and scaffolding
- Helps students understand concepts
- Positions itself as a **tutoring tool**, not a homework mill

---

## 🏗️ Core Features (Planned)

- **Socratic AI Tutor** – Guides students with questions and hints
- **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** – Grounds answers in the student’s own course materials
- **Multimodal Input** – Students can upload photos of homework problems
- **Multi-Agent Architecture**:
  - Teacher Agent (Socratic guidance)
  - Evaluator Agent (feedback on student work)
  - Orchestrator Agent (manages the conversation flow)
- **Freemium + B2B** business model
